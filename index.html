01 / Method

Learn together. Act locally.

Task coordination above
embodiment-specific locomotion.

01

Team-value-guided diffusion

Robot-specific diffusion policies learn flexible action distributions from reward-driven interaction through a variational objective, without demonstrations.

02

Robot-specific coordination

A centralized team-value function couples independent policies during training. Sequential updates account for evolving teammate policies.

03

Decentralized execution

Each robot uses local robot and payload observations without partner-state inputs or inter-robot messages. Frozen, embodiment-specific motion policies track its coordination commands.

02 / Perception

Onboard Payload Perception

The estimator combines onboard 3-D LiDAR and IMU measurements to recover the payload center and axis in each robot’s local frame. Reflective markers provide geometric constraints for association and filtering. Hardware deployment uses five markers; Figure 2 illustrates a three-band example.
